# QR AJN V5 Advanced Product Scope

QR AJN is two connected products:
1. Private, no-login mobile scanning and static QR creation.
2. Optional `qrajn.online` business identity, editable profiles, dynamic links and premium business tools.

Implemented V5 scope follows the uploaded final requirements: only Home/Scanner/Generator bottom navigation, Home Scan/Create cards, advanced scanner zoom and multiple-target selection, 30 category subpages, professional customization/export, Firebase profiles, website, ads and premium scaffolding.

Future enterprise extensions remain isolated in the Blaze backend: server redirects, analytics, team cards, event redemption, inventory, anti-counterfeit and verified subscription entitlements.
