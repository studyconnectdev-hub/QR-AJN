Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how QR AJN public profiles, QR workflows and account features work.

Create Business Profile Explore QR AJN

Does a customer need the QR AJN app?

No. Published public business profiles are designed to open in a normal browser.

Can a profile be updated after the QR is printed?

Yes. A business can keep the same public profile or dynamic QR route and update supported profile content or the dynamic destination later.

Is login required for every QR feature?

No. Account access is used for cloud business features such as owned profiles, dynamic links and analytics; public profile visitors do not need an account.

Can QR AJN use a custom domain?

Yes. The current web deployment is configured around qrajn.online and can be deployed through the included Vercel web setup.
