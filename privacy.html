QR AJN Privacy Policy

Effective: August 13, 2026

Local QR tools

Normal scanning and static QR creation are designed to work without an account. QR AJN does not intentionally keep a cloud history of raw scanned QR or barcode values, UPI PINs, Wi-Fi passwords, contact-card contents or precise GPS location.

Camera, gallery and generated files

Camera access is used to detect supported codes. Images selected with the system picker are processed for the requested scan. Generated files are saved or shared only when you choose those actions.

Optional business account

Signing in is required only for cloud business features such as editable profiles, media, dynamic QR links and owner analytics. Information you intentionally publish on a business profile is public at /b/<slug>.

Firebase and cloud services

QR AJN may use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Remote Config, App Check, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and optional Analytics. The production redirect and public-event backend runs on Google Cloud Run, while the public website can run on Vercel. Crash/performance services may process device, app and diagnostic information needed to operate the service.

Business analytics

Business analytics use aggregated counters such as profile views, contact actions and dynamic QR scans. For dynamic redirects, request headers may be processed transiently to derive privacy-minimized daily aggregates such as broad platform, hosting-provider country code when available, and broad referrer category. QR AJN does not intentionally persist raw IP addresses, full user-agent strings, destination URLs, precise GPS location or raw QR payloads in the business analytics collection.

Advertising and purchases

QR AJN 7.1.0 does not include active advertising or Play Billing. If monetization is introduced in a future release, this policy and the Play Data Safety declarations must be updated before those features are enabled.

Business media and leads

Profile photos, logos, cover images, gallery images and PDF brochures uploaded by a signed-in business owner are stored in Firebase Storage. Published business content is intentionally public. Do not publish confidential information.

UPI and external apps

QR AJN can parse supported payment requests and show details before opening an installed payment app. QR AJN never asks for or processes your UPI PIN and does not independently confirm payment success.

Safety reports

If you voluntarily report a suspicious destination, QR AJN may submit a one-way hash of the host, a reason category, schema version and timestamp. The full scanned URL path is not required for the standard report.

Retention and deletion

Signed-in users can unpublish or delete business content and can use the account deletion workflow. Operational logs and diagnostics may be retained for security, reliability or legal obligations according to the configured cloud services.

Security notice

SafeScan results are automated indicators, not guarantees. Always verify payment recipients, domain names and requested actions before continuing.
