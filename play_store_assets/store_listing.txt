APP NAME
QR AJN

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Scan safely, create custom QR codes and publish digital business profiles.

FULL DESCRIPTION
QR AJN is an advanced QR scanner, professional QR generator and digital business identity platform.

FAST, PRIVATE SCANNING
• Scan QR codes and supported barcodes with automatic focus and zoom
• Select a QR when several codes appear in the camera
• Scan from gallery images
• Review links and actions before opening
• No compulsory login and no automatic scan history

SAFE UPI AND SMART ACTIONS
• Review payee name, UPI ID, amount, currency and payment note
• Open PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM or another compatible UPI app
• Handle websites, deep links, Wi-Fi, contacts, calls, SMS, email, maps, social profiles, app links, tickets and structured QR data
• SafeScan warnings for suspicious destinations and risky file links

30 PROFESSIONAL QR CATEGORIES
Create QR codes for websites, text, Wi-Fi, UPI payments, contacts, locations, calendar events, business profiles, products, menus, price lists, reviews, coupons, event tickets, appointments, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, app downloads, deep links, multi-link pages, documents and more.

QR DESIGN STUDIO
• Professional gradients and color palettes
• Square, rounded, dot and diamond modules
• Captions, margin and error-correction controls
• Live preview and scan-quality score
• PNG sharing and download
• Premium SVG and PDF export

QRAJN.ONLINE BUSINESS PROFILES
Create an optional digital business profile with a public qrajn.online link. Add contact actions, products, services, social links, brochure, UPI payment and appointment links. Update a published profile without reprinting its QR code.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The free plan includes core scanning and QR creation with ads. QR AJN Pro removes ads and unlocks professional designs and exports. QR AJN Business adds cloud business-profile and advanced business tools.

Scan safely. Create professionally. Share your business instantly.
