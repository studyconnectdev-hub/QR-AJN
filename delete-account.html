Delete your QR AJN account

Open QR AJN, go to Settings → Delete business account and confirm. QR AJN first reauthenticates the signed-in account, then removes owner business media, business profiles, dynamic links, owner analytics/user data and finally the Firebase Authentication account.

If you cannot access the app, email support@qrajn.online from the registered email address. Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or payment information.

Data affected

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • Business profile and dynamic links
  • Owner analytics documents
  • Uploaded profile/logo/cover/gallery/brochure media owned by the account
  • User profile settings stored in Firestore

QR AJN 7.1.0 has no active advertising or Play Billing workflow.
