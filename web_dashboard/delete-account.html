Delete your QR AJN account

Open QR AJN, go to Settings → Delete business account, enter your current password and confirm. This removes the Firebase sign-in account and the business profile owned by that account.

If you cannot access the app, email support@qrajn.online from the registered email address. Include only the email address and public profile slug. Do not send passwords or payment information.

Data affected

  • Authentication account
  • Published business profile
  • User profile settings stored in Firestore

Google Play purchase records remain managed by Google Play and may be retained for billing, fraud prevention and legal obligations.
