QR AJN Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Privacy-first scanning: QR AJN does not require an account for normal scanning or static QR creation, and it does not maintain an automatic scan-history list.

Camera, gallery and generated files

Camera access is used to detect QR codes and supported barcodes. Images selected through the Android photo picker are processed for code detection. Generated PNG, SVG or PDF files are saved or shared only after the user chooses that action.

Optional QR AJN business account

An account is optional and is required only for cloud business profiles, editable links, premium entitlement synchronization and related business features. Account information may include an email address, display/profile details and profile content intentionally published by the user. Public profiles can be viewed through qrajn.online without requiring viewers to register.

Firebase

The application and website may use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging and optional Analytics. Raw scanned QR values, UPI PINs and Wi-Fi passwords are not intentionally sent to Firebase Analytics. Crash and performance services may process device, operating-system and diagnostic information needed to operate and improve the service.

Advertising

The free plan may display advertising through Google Mobile Ads. Ad delivery may process device identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions and diagnostics according to the user's consent choices, regional requirements and Google's policies. Premium plans remove in-app advertising.

Play Billing and premium plans

Purchases are processed by Google Play. QR AJN receives purchase identifiers and verification data required to activate or restore premium access. QR AJN does not receive the user's full card or bank details. Production purchase verification is performed by the optional secure backend when enabled.

Business profiles and leads

Information entered into a published business profile is intentionally public. Lead forms, appointment links, products, services, social links and contact information are controlled by the profile owner. Users should not publish confidential information.

UPI and external applications

QR AJN can parse UPI payment requests and show the payee, UPI ID, amount and note before opening an installed payment application. QR AJN never asks for or processes a UPI PIN and does not independently confirm payment success.

Community safety reports

If a user voluntarily reports a suspicious destination, QR AJN may submit a one-way hash of the host, a reason category and a timestamp. The full scanned path is not required for the standard report.

Retention and deletion

Local temporary scan information is cleared by the application lifecycle and is not presented as history. Signed-in users can delete or unpublish their business profile and can request account deletion through the application or support channel. Billing records may be retained where required for fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations.

Children

QR AJN is a general utility and business-profile service. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

Security notice

SafeScan results are automated indicators, not a guarantee. Users must verify payment recipients, domain names and requested actions before continuing.
